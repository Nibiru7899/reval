# Research

ReVal is also a research project: *can decision-level evidence freshness, applicability gating and cost-optimal restoration be combined, and does it help
compared with simpler baselines?* This page links the artifacts. Nothing here is a result unless it points to a script and a hash.

!!! warning "Status of the evidence"
    The prior-art review is a draft: all rows are `verified=no` until the owner reads the sources, and the pre-registration is unfilled. Benchmark results
    are only reportable when they come from a committed script with an archived result hash (`AGENTS.md`, rule 10). Undeclared physical drift (T9) is reported as
    a limitation, not as a result.

## How claims about performance are made

1. The change types and truth oracle are defined in advance in [06 Evaluation](../06-evaluation.md).
2. Primary metrics are ratios of sums with a stratified cluster bootstrap (strata = change type, clusters = base snapshot).
3. Hypotheses and exclusions are fixed in the pre-registration before the final run, with fresh seeds.
4. Every reported number is pulled from generated tables; none are hand-edited.
